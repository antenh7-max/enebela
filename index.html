Why Hawassa coffee is special

Hawassa sits in the heart of Sidama — one of the world's most celebrated coffee-growing regions. Here, coffee isn't just a drink, it's a culture.

Highland Grown

Sidama beans grow at 1,500–2,200m altitude for unmatched flavor complexity.

Buna Ceremony

The traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony — roasting, grinding, and brewing fresh jebena buna.

World-Renowned

Sidama coffee consistently ranks among the top-rated single origins globally.

Farm to Cup

Many Hawassa cafés source directly from local farmers — fresh, fair, and transparent.
